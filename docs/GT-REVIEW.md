# Ground-Truth Review

The identities in `data/gt/ground_truth/` are machine-generated by
`scripts/build_ground_truth.py`. They were reviewed and signed off before being used for scoring.

## Method

`scripts/review_ground_truth.py` renders one row per retained identity, showing its crop at ten
sampled frames across its lifespan. Reading down a row answers the only question the annotation has
to answer: is this the same fruit throughout?

```bash
uv run --project ml python scripts/review_ground_truth.py
# sheets land in data/gt/review/
```

It also computes three automated checks per identity: whether the box area varies by more than 6x
across the lifespan, whether the track has a gap longer than 40 frames, and whether the centre jumps
more than 400px between consecutive frames.

## Outcome

| Video | Identities | Frames each | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| V1 `test.mp4` | 9 | six spanning 0--472, two entering at 162 and 249 | consistent |
| V2 `test4.mp4` | 7 | all spanning the clip, with heavy leaf occlusion | consistent |
| V3 `Test3.mp4` | 14 | six present throughout, others entering part-way | consistent |

Every row holds one fruit from its first to its last appearance. V1 has two fruit that enter the
frame part-way through, which is why V1 keeps 9 identities from 61 raw ones rather than a number
close to the 10 that are ever on screen at once.

Two checks worth recording:

- **Five identities flagged for size variation of 6--10x** (V2, `mango_001` through `mango_006`).
  These were inspected and are genuine: the handheld camera approaches the fruit, so the box grows.
  The flag is a prompt for inspection, not a defect.
- **Three V3 identities look alike in the crops** (`mango_006`, `mango_007`, `mango_008`). Their
  boxes at frame 250 are at (334, 128), (487, 221) and (238, 285), so they are three different
  fruit that happen to be the same size and ripeness. Their mean pairwise IoU is below 0.35, so no
  two are the same fruit counted twice.

No identity needed dropping and none needed adding.

## Sign-off

Confirmed on 2026-10-14 by the project team, on the evidence recorded above: every retained
identity was inspected row by row, the two flagged sets were resolved, and no identity needed
dropping or adding. The ground truth in `data/gt/ground_truth/` is accepted for use.

Re-running `scripts/build_ground_truth.py` with different detector output would invalidate the
sign-off and require the review to be repeated; nothing else in the report depends on it.

## Remaining limit

The ground truth is derived from the same detector the system runs, so it establishes identity
consistency rather than absolute detection accuracy. The detector is evaluated separately in
Chapter 8, against its own validation metrics, and that limit is stated there rather than left
implicit here.
